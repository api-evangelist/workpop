# Workpop

> Workpop was a Santa Monica, CA hiring platform for hourly workers, backed by Obvious Ventures, Slow Ventures, and Trinity Ventures. It was acquired by Cornerstone OnDemand in 2018 and the product was shut down. Workpop never published a public API; its former domain (workpop.com) has lapsed and now redirects to an unrelated third-party site.
